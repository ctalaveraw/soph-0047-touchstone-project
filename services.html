Adoption Process

Our adoption process is designed to make sure every animal finds the right match. It begins with an online interest form, followed by a meet and greet, a home environment check, and a final adoption agreement. Most adoptions are completed within two weeks.

Foster Program

Foster families give animals a safe, comfortable place to stay while they wait for adoption. We provide all supplies, food, and veterinary coverage. Fostering can last anywhere from a few days to several months, depending on the animal's needs.

Volunteer Roles

  • Dog Walker: Provide daily exercise and enrichment for shelter dogs.
  • Cat Socializer: Spend time with shy or shelter-stressed cats to help them adjust.
  • Event Volunteer: Assist with adoption events and community outreach.
  • Administrative Support: Help with intake paperwork and phone inquiries.
