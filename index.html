Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue exists to give homeless and surrendered animals a second chance. We rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome pets across the metro area while connecting neighbors with volunteer and foster opportunities that make our work possible.

Our Impact

  • Over 1,200 animals placed in loving homes since 2018
  • 150 active foster families across the Twin Cities metro
  • 98% of rescued animals successfully rehabilitated

How You Can Help

Whether you have an hour a week or a spare bedroom for a foster pet, there is a role for you. Visit our Services page to learn more about adoption, fostering, and volunteering, or head to our Contact page to reach out directly.
